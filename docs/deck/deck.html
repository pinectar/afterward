Doctrine, not features

It never guesses

A server-side validator checks every reference against what the clerk actually said. Wrong captures are refused mid-call and re-asked. Anything the family didn't provide becomes an amber "Needs you" card. There is no code path that invents a value.

It says it's an AI

Every call opens with a disclosure that an AI assistant is calling with the family's permission — required by EU AI Act Art. 50 (in force 2 Aug 2026), and by decency first.

Evidence for everything

Every call ends with a stereo recording (institution left, agent right), a timeline, and a verified reference in a shareable Estate Ledger.

Proof05 / 10

Deep in the Voice Agent API

  • One live Voice Agent session per call — STT, turn detection, TTS, tools in one WebSocket
  • Client-side tools: press_key (DTMF), flag_needs_family, record_outcome — with a validator that can refuse
  • transcription_mode → max_accuracy switched mid-call, right before the reference is spoken
  • Per-call keyterms for the deceased's name — never the expected reference (no ground-truth biasing)
  • Sponsor-side stereo recordings (left = institution, right = agent) are the Ledger's evidence
  • Turn-taking survives scripted IVR menus, hold music and a 6-bit degraded-audio failure hunt

Market

Dozens
of organisations to notify per estate

Every death creates days of notification work. Funeral directors already sell aftercare and compete on service; estate solicitors bill for this by the hour. The category is proven — Empathy has raised over $90M, Settld partners with UK banks — and both stop at forms and email. Free services (Tell Us Once, DNS) cover slices and still leave the family holding the phone for the rest: what Afterward sells is the family’s time back, plus evidence — and the primary buyer is the funeral director, not the family.

Competitive analysis08 / 10

Business model & roadmap

Revenue
  • B2B (primary): funeral directors' aftercare packages — per-case licence, existing budget line, built-in distribution at the moment of need
  • B2B: estate & probate firms — per-matter, the Ledger drops straight into the file
  • B2C: per-estate, one price, unlimited institutions
Unit economics (COGS measured, prices are targets)

Measured: 12 calls = 27m45s agent time → ~2.3 min/call. Dozens of institutions ≈ ~70 min ≈ £2–3 COGS per estate → £79–149 per-case licence → at 650k UK deaths × 5% attach: £2.5–5M ARR.

Next 90 days
  • Real outbound leg (Twilio SIP ↔ Voice Agent API) — starting with requirements-discovery calls to real bereavement lines: "what do you need from a family?" — legitimate, harms no one, maps the terrain
  • Institution requirements directory built from those calls
  • Tell Us Once + DNS filing; document upload & tracking
